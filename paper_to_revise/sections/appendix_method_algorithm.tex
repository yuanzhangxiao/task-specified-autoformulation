\section{Autoformalism algorithm}
\label{app:af-algorithm}

Table~\ref{tab:af-algorithm} describes the full method, applied independently
to each prescribed seed and prompt variant. Component ablations disable only
their named scientific verifier, critic, shared-process stage, or pruning step;
parsing, execution safety, causal boundaries, and target completeness remain.
Every LLM request and numerical attempt is cached and checkpointed. Resume
reuses completed work and consumed budgets. Failures and unresolved checks
remain explicit in the reported denominators.

Let $E$ be validation NMSE from Eq.~\eqref{eq:af-output-loss}, and $K_t$ the
number of outer additive terms in state and process equations. Search prefers
smaller $E$ when parent and trial differ by more than
$\delta=10^{-8}+10^{-6}\max(|E_{\mathrm{parent}}|,|E_{\mathrm{trial}}|)$;
within $\delta$, it prefers smaller $K_t$, retaining the parent on a tie.
Pruning uses the count vector
$K_v=(\text{states},\text{processes},\text{parameters},\text{terms},
\text{expression nodes})$ and $E_b=\min(E_{\mathrm{parent}},E_{\mathrm{control}})$
when the control is available. It requires componentwise
$K_v(\mathrm{pruned})\le K_v(\mathrm{parent})$, at least one strict decrease,
and $E_{\mathrm{pruned}}\le E_b+\max(10^{-6},0.01E_b)$.
A failed control remains an incomplete paired comparison; a valid child can
still compete with the historical parent. In the table, $\operatorname{RMS}$
is the square root of the mean squared value over training-rollout samples.

7 & \qquad If no admissible draft remains, retain the incumbent with any

8 & \qquad Obtain or reuse the enabled critic's paired symbolic review

9 & \qquad Fit $\psi$, including initializer parameters, on training data

10 & \qquad Evaluate training and validation rollouts with frozen parameters

11 & \qquad Select the eligible finite candidate by validation error, then

13 & \quad \textbf{If} pruning is enabled and an incumbent is available:\\
14 & \qquad Enumerate legal deletions preserving outputs, causality and

15 & \qquad On the parent's training rollout, compute

16 & \qquad Delete that unit and unreachable declarations; recheck validity.
 Refit the child and unchanged parent with equal budgets. A skipped deletion
 can still receive its prescribed control.\\
17 & \qquad Select parent, control or child by the rule above. Review changed
 selected equations with the enabled critic, without altering selection.\\
18 & Report every endpoint, including failures. Freeze selected models;
 only then evaluate held-out test data without refitting or model changes.\\
