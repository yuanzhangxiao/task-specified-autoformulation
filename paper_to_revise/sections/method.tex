\section{Method}
\label{sec:method}
\label{sec:autoformalism-method}

Autoformalism combines staged language-model proposals, deterministic checks,
numerical fitting, and pruning. MAP motivates the formulation; bounded search
and validation-based selection implement its practical approximation.

\subsection{MAP-motivated model search}
\label{sec:af-map}

Write $M=(\Gamma,f)$ for the model structure, including observation and
initialization rules, and $\psi$ for its continuous parameters. MAP maximizes
$p(D\mid M,\psi)p(M,\psi\mid\mathcal C)$ given observations $D$ and context
$\mathcal C$. We approximate the unknown context-conditioned prior by
\begin{equation}
 p_{\mathrm{work}}(M,\psi\mid\mathcal C)
 \propto
 \mathbf 1\{M\in\mathcal A_{\mathcal C},\,\psi\in\Theta_M\}
 e^{-\lambda K(M)},
 \label{eq:af-prior}
\end{equation}
on bounded structure and parameter domains. Here $\mathcal A_{\mathcal C}$
encodes the implemented admissibility requirements, $\Theta_M$ the parameter
domains, and $K(M)$ structural complexity. Writing
$L_D=-\log p(D\mid M,\psi)$, taking negative logarithms gives
\begin{equation}
 \min_{M\in\mathcal A_{\mathcal C},\,\psi\in\Theta_M}
 L_D(M,\psi)+\lambda K(M).
 \label{eq:af-map-surrogate}
\end{equation}
Choosing the prior is a modeling approximation; the logarithmic reformulation
is exact. Admissibility covers executable requirements, not complete scientific
correctness. Explicit violations block acceptance when verification is enabled;
unresolved predicates remain reported and may remain eligible for search.

\subsection{Staged model construction}
\label{sec:af-construction}

The proposer first selects observed and latent variables, optionally proposes
shared processes, specifies signed dependency hyperedges, and supplies interaction
functions. A shared process has one defining function and parameter set, reused
by its consumers, with $\mathcal P_i$ indexing processes used by equation $i$:
\begin{equation}
 F_i=\sum_j T_{ij}+\sum_{p\in\mathcal P_i}s_{ip}a_{ip}d_{ip}P_p.
 \label{eq:af-process}
\end{equation}
Here $T_{ij}$ are ordinary contributions, $P_p$ a shared law,
$s_{ip}$ a declared sign, $a_{ip}$ a consumer gain, and $d_{ip}$ a declared
conversion factor. Explicit transfer declarations can tie gains; independent
effective gains permit unknown conversions without claiming conservation.
The runtime propagates shared-law edits to every consumer. Optional process
failure permits ordinary construction to continue.

Typed schemas and a restricted expression grammar enforce symbol closure,
causality, and algebraic acyclicity. Equation-derived checks assess applicable
public paths, dynamic mediation, and nonlinear feedback. Unambiguous repairs
are automatic; scientific ambiguities return to the proposer with the current
model and diagnostics.
Latent states use declared causal initializers. Rollouts generate subsequent
outputs without resetting states from later target observations.

\subsection{Fitting and revision}
\label{sec:af-revision}

For target channel $q$, fix its scale $\sigma_q$ from training observations.
Let $e_{rkq}(M,\psi)=(\widehat y_{rq}(t_k;M,\psi)-y_{rq}(t_k))/\sigma_q$
be the standardized residual for trajectory $r$ at time $t_k$.
A Gaussian working likelihood yields the inner fitting problem
\begin{equation}
 \widehat\psi_M\approx
 \arg\min_{\psi\in\Theta_M}\frac12
 \sum_{(r,k,q)\in\mathcal I_{\mathrm{tr}}}e_{rkq}(M,\psi)^2,
 \label{eq:af-fit}
\end{equation}
where $\mathcal I_{\mathrm{tr}}$ indexes training observations.
Collocation initialization and rollout-based refinement operate within fixed
budgets. Because $K(M)$ is structural, fitting contains no sparsity penalty.
Validation NMSE $E_{\mathrm{val}}$ is the mean squared standardized residual with
training scales and fitted parameters, including initializer parameters, frozen.
Validation-based structural selection is a predictive surrogate, not an exact
MAP identity.

The paired scientific critic supplies symbolic concerns $c_t$ from the public
context and candidate equations, without trajectories or numerical scores.
Alongside training-residual feedback $r_t$, these condition the proposer:
\begin{equation}
 M'_t\sim q_\phi(M\mid\mathcal C,M_t,r_t,c_t).
 \label{eq:af-critic-proposal}
\end{equation}
Revisions can jointly change variables, topology, laws, and initializers.
The runtime recompiles and fits each candidate. Among eligible finite candidates,
lower validation NMSE wins outside a fixed numerical tolerance; within it, fewer
additive terms wins, otherwise the incumbent is retained. This approximates an
error-first, complexity-second policy, motivated by $\lambda\to0^+$ over a
finite candidate set. Critic scores neither select nor veto models;
the critic guides the search distribution rather than supplying a posterior
energy. Test data remain inaccessible until selection is frozen.

\subsection{Process-aware pruning}
\label{sec:af-pruning}

Pruning tests one legal deletion with the lowest training-rollout contribution,
measured by its RMS relative to its defining right-hand side. A shared-process
deletion removes all consumer contributions together and uses their maximum
ratio. Deletion precedes refitting; unreachable components are removed.

The pruned model and an unchanged control receive equal refitting budgets.
Let $E_b$ be the better available validation NMSE of the parent and control.
Accept the deletion only when applicable public checks pass, no count of states,
processes, parameters, terms, or expression nodes increases, at least one decreases,
and
\begin{equation}
 E_{\mathrm{pruned}}\le E_b+\max\{10^{-6},0.01E_b\}.
 \label{eq:af-pruning-accept}
\end{equation}
Unresolved obligations skip deletion when verification is enabled. Pruning
approximates structural regularization locally. These policies are not claimed
to exactly optimize a single fixed-weight MAP objective.
