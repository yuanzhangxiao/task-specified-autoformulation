\section{Method}
\label{sec:method}
\label{sec:autoformalism-method}

Autoformalism searches over $\mathcal M=(\Gamma,f,h,g;\psi)$ using staged
LLM proposals, deterministic verification, scientific critique, numerical
fitting, and pruning. These components implement a bounded, MAP-motivated
search; they do not exactly optimize a known posterior.

\subsection{From MAP to practical model search}

Taking negative logarithms of the MAP objective in
Section~\ref{sec:formulation} gives
\begin{equation}
 \min_{\mathcal M}\;
 \underbrace{-\log p(\mathcal D\mid\mathcal M)}_{\mathcal L_{\mathrm{data}}}
 -\log p(\mathcal M\mid\mathcal C).
 \label{eq:af-map-energy}
\end{equation}
We use a Gaussian working likelihood for target prediction errors,
conditional on supplied inputs and permitted initial information.
For split $\mathcal D_s$, its normalized squared-error counterpart is
\begin{equation}
 E_s(\mathcal M)=\frac{1}{|\mathcal I_s|}
 \sum_{(n,k,q)\in\mathcal I_s}
 \left[
 \frac{\widehat y_{n,q}(t_{n,k};\mathcal M)-y_{n,q}(t_{n,k})}
 {\sigma_q}
 \right]^2,
 \label{eq:af-output-loss}
\end{equation}
where $\widehat{\mathbf y}_n=h(\widehat{\mathbf x}_n,\mathbf u_n;\psi)$
is obtained by integrating $f$ from $g(\boldsymbol\zeta_n;\psi)$.
The index set $\mathcal I_s$ contains target-channel samples, and
$\sigma_q>0$ is the training standard deviation with a numerical floor,
reused on every split. Thus fitting compares predicted outputs, rather
than substituting state coordinates for a general observation function $h$.

We approximate the structural preference in the unknown prior using
$p_{\mathrm{work}}(\mathcal M\mid\mathcal C)\propto
\mathbf 1\{\mathcal M\in\mathcal A_{\mathcal C}\}
\exp[-\lambda K(\mathcal M)]$, on the permitted model and parameter domains.
Here $\mathcal A_{\mathcal C}$ denotes implemented admissibility checks
and $K$ structural complexity. After absorbing likelihood scaling into
$\lambda$, this yields the surrogate
$\min_{\mathcal M\in\mathcal A_{\mathcal C}}
E_{\mathrm{tr}}(\mathcal M)+\lambda K(\mathcal M)$.
For a fixed structure, $K$ is constant, motivating an inner parameter fit.
In practice, we fit $\psi$ on training data, select
structures by validation error with complexity preferences, and use
scientific critique to guide proposals. This predictive selection and
local pruning approximate the MAP tradeoff without evaluating an LLM
score as a log prior or minimizing one fixed weighted objective.

\subsection{Staged construction and graph meta models}

The proposer selects variables $\Gamma$, optionally declares shared
processes, specifies signed dependencies, and supplies analytic
interaction functions, output mappings $h$, and causal initializers $g$.
A graph meta model (GMM) represents dependencies as directed hyperedges:
a set of state/input variables jointly contributes one function to a
state derivative or algebraic definition. Shared processes have one
law and parameter registry, reused across their consumers. Declared
signs, conversions, and parameter sharing are preserved during assembly
and coherent revision; shared syntax alone does not establish conservation.

For a block of eligible outer weights $\theta$, the assembled dynamics
can be written, with all remaining parameters collected in $\eta$, as
\begin{equation}
 f_i(\mathbf x,\mathbf u;\psi)
 =b_i(\mathbf x,\mathbf u;\eta)
 +\sum_{j\in\mathcal E_i}\theta_{\kappa(i,j)}
 \phi_{i,j}(\mathbf x_{S_{i,j}},\mathbf u;\eta),
 \label{eq:af-gmm}
\end{equation}
where $\psi=(\theta,\eta)$, $b_i$ collects the remaining contributions,
and $\kappa$ preserves tied weight identities. Basis functions may be
nonlinear in their arguments and in $\eta$. We explain the resulting
conditional analytical solution below.

\subsection{Deterministic validity and scientific critique}

\textbf{Validity gate.} Before fitting, the runtime compiles the proposed
model and checks a deterministic rubric: restricted expressions and
symbol closure, complete generated targets and output mappings,
algebraic acyclicity, causal initializers, and declared parameter/sign
contracts. Equation-derived public requirements additionally check, when
specified, input--target pathways, mediation through dynamic states,
and nonlinear source or feedback dependence. Explicit violations trigger
bounded repair; a model that still violates an enforced requirement is
not fitted. The gate is operational admissibility, not certification of
all scientific claims: unresolved scientific predicates remain reported
and can remain development-eligible under the declared policy.

\textbf{Scientific critic.} Beyond these executable checks, a judge LLM
assesses the plausibility of the proposed mechanisms, variable meanings,
and interpretation in context. We reuse the paired GPT-OSS-120B judge:
atomic checks inform a comparative review in both candidate orders.
It sees the public specification and symbolic models, without fitted
values or prediction scores. Its findings provide fallible scientific
advice for subsequent revisions; its score neither selects nor vetoes
a model and cannot override the validity gate. Unchanged symbolic
reviews are cached. This separates mechanically checkable requirements
from broader scientific interpretation that our deterministic rubric
does not establish.

\subsection{Conditional linearity and numerical fitting}

\textbf{What can be solved analytically.} Fix the state trajectories and
all parameters $\eta$, including those inside nonlinear laws. If the
expanded equations are affine in the chosen weights, stacking their
basis evaluations gives a design matrix $\Phi$ and derivative targets
$d=\operatorname{vec}(\dot X-b)$, where $X$ stacks state values on a
fixed time grid. The conditional problem is
\begin{equation}
 \theta^\star(X,\eta)\in
 \arg\min_{\theta\in\Theta_\theta}
 \|\Phi(X,\eta)\theta-d(X,\eta)\|_2^2.
 \label{eq:af-conditional-linear}
\end{equation}
For unrestricted weights, a minimum-norm solution is
\begin{equation}
 \theta_{\mathrm{LS}}(X,\eta)
 =\Phi(X,\eta)^\dagger d(X,\eta),
 \label{eq:af-conditional-solution}
\end{equation}
where $\dagger$ denotes the Moore--Penrose pseudoinverse.
Sign or bound constraints instead require constrained linear least
squares. Products of unknown gains across nested shared laws must be
excluded from the same affine block. Crucially, $d$ contains fixed
trajectory derivatives, not the observed outputs $\mathbf Y$;
this conditional solution does not make rollout error quadratic in
$\psi$ or solve the complete partially observed fitting problem.

\textbf{Implemented fitter.} The current pipeline jointly fits the
continuous parameters with an existing collocation initializer and
bounded rollout-based refinement; it does not alternate analytical
weight solves. Collocation estimates discretized state trajectories
and parameters under the dynamical and initial-condition constraints.
Refinement minimizes training output residuals through forward
simulation, using the estimated parameters rather than treating
collocation nodes as latent observations. Parameters of $f$, $h$, and
$g$ are fitted together, including unknown constant latent initials or
parameters of causal initial maps. They remain fixed for validation
and test rollouts, which never reset states from later target values.
There is no sparsity penalty in this fitting objective.

\subsection{Revision, selection, and pruning}

The proposer receives the current model, deterministic diagnostics,
qualified training-residual feedback, and available critic findings.
It can jointly revise $\Gamma,f,h,g$ within fixed budgets. Each new
model passes the validity gate before review and fitting. Among eligible
finite fits, lower validation error wins outside a fixed numerical
tolerance; within it, fewer additive terms wins, otherwise the incumbent
is retained. The critic influences which candidates are explored,
not their numerical ranking.

After search, pruning tests one legal deletion ranked by its contribution
on the retained training rollout: the RMS of an additive contribution
relative to its defining right-hand side. Coupled deletions use the
largest ratio across consumers. Shared-law edits propagate to all uses,
and unreachable components are removed. The pruned model and an
unchanged control receive equal fitting budgets. A pruned model is
retained only if the checks pass, complexity decreases without increasing
any tracked count, and its validation NMSE is within a predeclared
$1\%$ tolerance (absolute floor $10^{-6}$) of the better parent/control.
Unresolved public obligations skip automatic deletion. These local
simplifications implement the preference for parsimony. Final models
are frozen before test evaluation; Table~\ref{tab:af-algorithm} gives
the algorithm and exact selection rules.
