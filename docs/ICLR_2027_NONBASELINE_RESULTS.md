# Scientific evidence and pending discovery runs

Both retained six-visit Full models predicted the direction of the T2-hard glucose response to a 48 g meal. Their relative squared response errors were **0.481 and 0.053** for seeds 0 and 1. The same models gave insulin-dependent disposal response errors of **0.999 and 1.637** on a short fasting insulin pulse, using a latent-coordinate probe calibrated on training schedules. Predicting zero change scores 1. These development experiments support a claim about meal-response prediction. Recovery of hidden insulin action across interventions remains unestablished.
