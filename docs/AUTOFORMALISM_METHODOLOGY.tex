\section{Method}
\label{sec:autoformalism-method}

Autoformalism constructs task-sufficient dynamical models from a scientific
specification and partially observed trajectories. A language-model proposer
supplies variables and mechanisms; a deterministic runtime assembles and checks
the equations; numerical fitting estimates their parameters. Bounded revision
uses training residuals, while validation selects among candidates. Shared
processes, pruning, and scientific critique are configurable modules; each
experiment specifies which are enabled.

\subsection{Model and information boundary}
\label{sec:af-model}

Given permitted inputs $u(t)$, fixed covariates $c$, and observed outputs $y(t)$,
a candidate $\mathcal M$ defines
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
 \dot{x}&=F_{\mathcal M}(t,x,u,c;\theta),\\
 \widehat y&=H_{\mathcal M}(t,x,u,c;\theta).
 \end{aligned}
 \label{eq:af-model}
\end{equation}
The state $x$ may include unobserved variables, and named algebraic processes
form an acyclic dependency graph. Parameters $\theta$ are shared across
trajectories. Observed states start from their measured initial values; latent
states use declared constants, shared fitted values, or causal maps of available
initial observations, inputs, and covariates. Any initializer coefficients are
fitted on training data and fixed for validation and testing. Predictions are
open rollouts: subsequent output measurements neither reset states nor serve as
forcing. Test trajectories and metrics remain inaccessible until model selection
and the protocol are frozen.

\subsection{Staged model construction}
\label{sec:af-construction}

Construction proceeds through variable selection, optional shared-process
proposal, topology, and interaction functions. Topology specifies signed
hyperedges from sets of drivers to equation targets. The interaction stage
supplies a function for each declared dependency. The \textit{full} variant also
receives descriptive training-trajectory summaries during initial construction;
\textit{brief-only} omits these summaries. Both use the same subsequent fitting
and residual-feedback procedure.

A shared process declares its drivers, consumers, contribution signs, scientific
meaning, and any known conversions. Its function $P_p$ and internal parameters
are defined once and referenced by every consumer:
\begin{equation}
 F_i=\sum_j T_{ij}^{\mathrm{ordinary}}
       +\sum_{p\in\mathcal P_i}s_{ip}g_{ip}d_{ip}P_p.
 \label{eq:af-process}
\end{equation}
Here $\mathcal P_i$ indexes processes consumed by equation $i$,
$s_{ip}\in\{-1,+1\}$ is the declared sign, $g_{ip}\geq0$ a fitted gain,
and $d_{ip}$ a declared conversion factor. A declared transfer with complete
conversions uses opposite signs and a common gain; a shared influence uses
separate consumer gains. When conversions are unknown, effective independent
gains absorb the missing conversions, without imposing conservation. The runtime
owns outer signs and gain assembly and propagates changes to the shared law
across its consumers. Empty or invalid optional process suggestions allow ordinary
construction to continue.

All proposals pass typed schemas and a restricted expression grammar. The runtime
checks symbol closure, causal initialization, algebraic acyclicity, and public
requirements derived from the equations, including applicable input--target
paths, dynamic mediation, and nonlinear-feedback predicates. Explicit violations
block acceptance. Unresolved predicates remain labelled as such; the development
policy can retain these candidates without treating them as certified passes.
Unambiguous representation errors are normalized mechanically; ambiguous changes
to scientific content return to the proposer. Coordinated edits commit atomically,
and repair prompts show the actual current model and failed checks.

\subsection{Bounded numerical fitting}
\label{sec:af-fitting}

For output $q$, let $\sigma_q$ be its pooled training standard deviation, floored
at $10^{-8}$. Define the residual at time $t_k$ of trajectory $r$ by
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
 e_{rkq}(\theta)&=
 \frac{\widehat y_{rq}(t_k;\theta)-y_{rq}(t_k)}{\sigma_q},\\
 E_D&=\frac{1}{N_D}\sum_{(r,k,q)\in\mathcal I_D}e_{rkq}^{\,2},
 \end{aligned}
 \label{eq:af-loss}
\end{equation}
where $\mathcal I_D$ indexes the $N_D$ scalar output observations in split $D$.
Fitting minimizes
$\frac12\sum_{\mathrm{train}}e_{rkq}^{\,2}$ within fixed time and evaluation
budgets. NMSE $E_D$ measures output trajectories, with no latent-state error term;
validation uses the training scales and fixed fitted parameters.

We initialize by collocation, jointly optimizing parameters and trajectory nodes
under the model equations and causal boundary conditions. Only parameters pass
to subsequent initial-value simulations. Training-rollout screening precedes
bounded least-squares refinement with forward-sensitivity derivatives; supported
nonsmooth cases have a bounded exact-rollout polling fallback. The best finite
training-evaluated point is retained. Numerical termination, budget exhaustion,
and predictive error are reported separately: an unfinished optimization does
not establish that the proposed structure cannot fit the observations.

\subsection{Feedback, revision, and selection}
\label{sec:af-revision}

At retained parameters, training residuals identify where mismatch occurs over
time and across input conditions. The proposer receives these patterns, numerical
status, deterministic findings, and the complete current model. It may jointly
revise states, dependencies, laws, shared processes, and initializers. These are
conditional observations about a fitted candidate, not diagnoses proving a missing
mechanism. Accepted revisions are recompiled and fitted using compatible parent
parameter values. Among candidates with eligible public checks and complete
finite rollouts, selection minimizes validation NMSE, breaking ties by additive
term count and retaining the incumbent on an exact tie. A fixed round budget
bounds each lineage.

An optional scientific critic reuses the established GPT-OSS-120B paired judge.
Its two stages assess sign-blinded atomic contributions and broader symbolic
consistency in both candidate orderings. The judge receives public requirements
and symbolic models, without trajectories, fitted values, or numerical scores.
Consensus concerns are supplied as separately labelled advice alongside
deterministic and numerical feedback. Reviews use the actual model after pruning
or revision and before any new fit. Critic preferences neither select nor veto
candidates and cannot override deterministic failures. This advisory role supplies
revision hypotheses, rather than scientific certification.

\subsection{Process-aware pruning}
\label{sec:af-pruning}

Pruning removes one weak legal contribution from a retained model. An ordinary
deletion unit is a complete signed additive term. Removing a shared process
removes all its consumer contributions together; editing its defining law
propagates to all consumers. Ambiguous deletions are excluded, observation
mappings are preserved, and unreachable states, processes, parameters, and
initializers are cleaned up. Automatic deletion is skipped when public obligations
are unresolved.

Write a defining right-hand side as $F_i=\sum_jT_{ij}$. On the parent's training
rollouts at recorded times, compute
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
 r_{ij}&=\frac{\operatorname{RMS}(T_{ij})}
                  {\max\{\operatorname{RMS}(F_i),10^{-12}\}},\\
 r(U)&=\max_{(i,j)\in U}r_{ij}.
 \end{aligned}
 \label{eq:af-pruning-rank}
\end{equation}
Here $T_{ij}$ is the entire signed contribution, including fitted coefficients,
and $\operatorname{RMS}(T)=\sqrt{N^{-1}\sum_{n=1}^N T(t_n)^2}$ pools training
samples. A deletion unit $U$ is ranked by its largest affected-term ratio. The
lowest-ranked legal unit is fixed before refitting or inspecting new validation
scores. This is one greedy deletion, not an exhaustive sparsity search.

We refit the pruned model and an unchanged control with equal allocations and
compatible parent warm starts. Let $E_b$ be the better available validation NMSE
of the retained parent and refitted control. The pruned model is accepted only
if public checks pass, no component of the complexity vector increases, at least
one decreases, and
\begin{equation}
 E_{\mathrm{pruned}}\leq E_b+\max\{10^{-6},0.01E_b\}.
 \label{eq:af-pruning-accept}
\end{equation}
Complexity counts states, named processes, fitted parameters, additive terms,
and expression-tree nodes. The tolerance is a declared simplification policy,
not a scientific threshold. Prompts, replies, fitted checkpoints, decisions,
and call/token usage are recorded for reproducible, bounded resume.
