## Ewor — What changes in the search, and do the learned mechanisms extrapolate?

We agree that the value of searching for a state representation needs a direct measurement. Our original six-visit archive already shows substantial seed dependence. We could reconstruct both executable equations for 38 of 80 planned seed pairs. Sixteen pairs shared a state dimension. No pair had identical signed connectivity or an identical equation fingerprint after canonical renaming. Different equations can still implement equivalent dynamics. We include a successful T2-hard search and an interrupted T2-easy search, with the candidate and retained equations, fitted coefficients, validation errors and the coverage at each visit. The archive omits the verbatim critic replies. [Worked lineages](../search-history/candidate_lineages.md) · [All seed pairs](../search-history/seed_stability.csv)

We also froze historical models and measured the change from each model's own control under independent interventions. The two Full seeds predicted the direction of the glucose response to a 48 g meal, with relative squared errors of 0.481 and 0.053. Under a short fasting insulin pulse, a training-calibrated latent-coordinate probe for insulin-dependent disposal scored 0.999 and 1.637. A prediction of zero response scores 1. In one comparison matched on state and parameter counts, two Brief-only candidates had validation NMSEs of 1.015 and 0.975. Their disposal-response errors on a long fed insulin pulse were 25.97 and 4.48. Similar validation performance therefore allowed materially different extrapolations in this pair. The table includes every pair meeting the stated development calipers. [Paired effects](../fidelity/intervention_effects.csv) · [Development-matched pairs](../fidelity/matched_fit_pairs.csv)

Our supplied-equation controls quantify the performance attainable with strong structural knowledge. We fitted shared parameters and initializers on training trajectories, selected among three starts on validation, and then evaluated interventions. A canonical glucose structure gave validation NMSE of about 3.68 × 10⁻¹⁰ on canonical T1-easy and 0.865 on perturbed T1-easy after refitting. The experiment varies one exchange law and uses privileged structure and initialization scales. We have no human study measuring expert modeling time. [Reference-fit results](../reference/reference_structure_results.csv) · [Canonical structure transfer](../reference/literature_transfer_results.csv)

## pp8W — What happens with less information, larger systems, or a wrong specification?

We separated measurement noise, sampling frequency and sensor choice. The clean and degraded observations use the same underlying forcing schedules. As a fitting check, we supplied the true structure and fitted it to T2 data with 10% measurement noise. The selected model had validation NMSE of 0.0101 against the noisy measurements and 5.66 × 10⁻⁵ against their clean counterparts. All three starts reached the 50-evaluation cap. End-to-end discovery under these conditions remains unmeasured. The scaling design uses two procedural draws each at total dimensions 4, 8 and 12 and records all rejected draws. Each retained hidden coordinate passed the declared excitation and observable-influence tests. Two draws per dimension support a bounded sensitivity study within this generator family. [Acquisition design](../stress/data_robustness_manifest.json) · [Condition-specific fits](../stress/conditioned-references/reference_structure_results.csv) · [Scaling design](../stress/scaling_system_manifest.json)

For the noiseless reactor we can test a specific wrong assumption directly. If jacket-feed temperature cannot affect reactor temperature, deterministic models with the same preparation and the same remaining input histories must predict equal temperature trajectories. We found ten such training pairs. Every pair violated that equality, with a maximum difference of 25.776 K. This establishes an observable contradiction under the stated preparation. Detecting it automatically during search is a separate experiment. Glucose trajectories alone give a weaker diagnostic because competing hidden changes can produce similar outputs. [Reactor training pairs and numerical tolerances](../specification/reactor_training_pairs.csv)

## 7pHK — What is imposed, what was recovered, and what do the submitted tables measure?

The task supplies channel roles and qualitative pathway requirements. The modeler must choose an executable state representation, equations, readouts and causal initializers, and fit continuous parameters. Our operational compliance rubric was developed after preliminary results. It measures satisfaction of the specified requirements. For evidence about physical behavior we now examine hidden trajectories and matched intervention responses, including failures and unavailable correspondences. The correct meal-response direction and the weak disposal recovery reported above illustrate the difference. A hidden-coordinate rescaling can preserve every observed trajectory. We restrict the recovery claim to the functions and interventions actually measured. [Hidden-process outcomes](../fidelity/hidden_process_metrics.csv) · [Identifiability and related work](ICLR_2027_IDENTIFIABILITY.md)

Seven of the nine submitted cases derive from one glucose simulator. In T1-easy, several physiologic terms are supplied as auxiliaries, including tissue glucose. The perturbation changes plasma–tissue exchange. For the six-visit Full configuration, the pooled validation medians over the two prompt variants and two seeds were 0.397 and 0.416 on the named and obfuscated original cases, and 0.170 and 0.362 on the corresponding perturbed cases. These comparisons test sensitivity to names and to that change in dynamics. They cannot determine what an LLM encountered in pretraining. Recomputing the generator at forcing boundaries changed 11 training, three validation and three test schedules above tolerance.
