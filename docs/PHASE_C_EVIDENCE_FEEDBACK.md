# Phase C: public coverage and conditional training feedback

The opt-in `minimal-evidence-feedback-1` policy tests three changes together in
40 fresh constructions: eight public cases, Full specification, seeds 0–4.
Historical policies, finalized benchmark prompts and datasets are unchanged.
This is a construction study, with no interaction fitting or test-data access.

## Coverage versus scientific evidence

The previous four meal records cited only a supplied input even though their
meal-to-target paths existed. Source-only records remain provisional during
variables. At topology completion, each mechanism record must also cite an
existing generated variable or shared process implementing the effect. Feedback
separates this missing attribution from a genuinely missing graph path. It does
not introduce another state, law or scientific association automatically.

A separate reviewed, quote-bound public contract requires identification of the
downstream external discharge in the two basin cases. The proposer selects an
ordinary contribution by its receiver and zero-based local index, or a shared
process use. Equivalent storage coordinates with algebraic target readouts are
allowed. A modeled internal transfer cannot stand in for a distinct external
loss. The runtime binds the reference to its exact contribution hash; subsequent
edits cannot silently retarget it. This checks declared role coverage, not the
truth of that role, quantitative sign, threshold behavior or conservation.

## Training observations and questions

Complete public training arrays are exported to a sealed evidence bundle. The
exporter checks source hashes against the historical bounded training evidence,
and seals complete auxiliary and covariate arrays as well. It opens only explicit
public `train.json` and `specification.json` files. The prompt retains the old
public packet and adds at most 3,500 characters of factual highlights: matched
recorded inputs with different outputs, changes under constant inputs, and
sampled extrema. Equalities use original values; display rounding and peak times
are not causal conclusions. An explicit stage instruction asks the proposer to
compare its choices with these observations.

After topology, the existing three qualified diagnostics become advisory repair
questions: single-valued algebraic maps, uniqueness of a closed dynamic subsystem,
and reversal in a scalar autonomous subsystem. Questions show actual trajectory
IDs, times, observations, conditioning and assumptions. Unknown latent initial
conditions, forcing or other hypotheses lead to deferral. Compiler/model-class
hypotheses are explicit assumptions, not facts inferred from data. Conflicts
concern exact recorded reproduction; they do not prove unacceptable fitted NMSE.

The proposer may revise dependencies, explain an intended approximation, or
dispute an assumption. These answers are reply controls, kept separately from
scientific variables. Only displayed question IDs may be answered. Questions
are bound to the topology and evidence; a changed candidate is reassessed.
Unanswered questions receive another opportunity within the existing repair
reserve. Structural eligibility does not depend on a zero conflict count, and
reports separately show advice status, retained approximations and disputed
assumptions. An explanation is not verification.

## Budgets, artifacts and next check

The previous 133-request / 655,360-token episode limits remain, with ten global
repair calls / 262,144 tokens reserved, checkpoints after five and ten calls,
and the existing semantic-repetition stop. There are no extra unbounded reviews.
Calls, displayed witnesses, accepted answers, exact drafts and assessments are
cached and sealed for deterministic resume. Full evidence stays outside prompts.

Signs of complete functions, overlapping effects and quantitative conservation
remain interaction-stage work. Explicit boundary roles are carried into the
handoff. This milestone neither claims those checks have run nor chooses an
unavailable function on the proposer's behalf.

Inspect all seeds and stage exits in the next run. Compare structural coverage,
whether the formerly isolated subsystems change, explanations for retained
approximations, and actual equations. A higher completion count alone does not
establish better scientific models; these combined changes are not a causal
ablation against the earlier prompt policy.

## Running the confirmation

The delivery archive contains pinned source, the unchanged compatibility requests,
original public topology source plan, and the complete training evidence bundle.
Use the existing verified Jetstream2 H100 environment; no new environment or model
installation is required. From the extracted delivery directory on that VM:

```bash
export AF_JS2_ROOT="$HOME/af-construction"
export AF_JS2_CAMPAIGN_ROOT="$AF_JS2_ROOT/evidence-feedback-seeds5-v1"
export AF_JS2_TRAINING_EVIDENCE="$PWD/inputs/training-evidence.json"
sudo -v
bash scripts/jetstream/run_evidence_feedback_construction.sh run
```

Use the same directory and environment for `status` and, once stopped, `inspect`.
Rerunning `run` resumes the same frozen experiment and cached calls. Changing the
source, evidence or configuration requires a new experiment directory. The server
must be running; the launcher reports a specific restart instruction if it is not.
The new wrapper preserves the prior construction workflows.

The inspection archive includes complete evidence in the frozen plan, prompt and
response records, stage reports, compatibility assessments and advisory answers.
Check `status_counts` and `training_compatibility` in `summary.json` together;
`topology_complete` alone is not a verdict on the training conflicts.
